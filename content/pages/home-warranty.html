How home warranty service works

Your home warranty company and Kappa-Air work as a team. Here's what to expect from the moment you file a claim to the moment your air is cold again.

  1. File your claim with your warranty company. Most plans let you file online or by phone. Describe what the system is doing (or not doing) as best you can.
  2. Your warranty company sends a contractor. If they assign Kappa-Air, we'll reach out to schedule your visit. You can also call us at {{PHONE}}.
  3. We diagnose the problem. Our technician inspects the system, finds the cause, and explains what we found in plain language.
  4. We report to your warranty company. We send them what they need to review your claim: the equipment's model and serial numbers, the failed part, and the repair or replacement it calls for.
  5. Your warranty company authorizes the work. Coverage decisions belong to your warranty company and the terms of your plan. We'll let you know what they approve.
  6. We get you comfortable again. Once the work is authorized, we order any parts and complete the repair or replacement.

Tips for a smooth claim

  • Keep your plan handy. Your contract spells out what's covered, any limits, and the service call fee for each visit.
  • Keep up with maintenance. Many equipment manufacturers require regular maintenance to keep their warranties valid, and a well-maintained system is less likely to break down in the first place. See our maintenance services.
  • Know your equipment. If you can, note the brand and model number from the label on your indoor and outdoor units. It helps everyone move faster.
  • Describe the symptoms. Warm air, strange noises, water leaks, or a system that won't turn on at all: the more detail you give, the better prepared we arrive.

Questions about coverage?

Your warranty company decides what your plan covers, so they're the best people to ask about coverage, limits, and fees. When we're at your home, we're always glad to explain what we found and how it relates to the repair your warranty company is reviewing.

Something your plan doesn't cover? If we find a problem outside your coverage, we'll explain it before any work is done, and the decision is always yours.
