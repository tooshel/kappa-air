10 common air conditioning problems

  • Faulty thermostat. If your thermostat stops working correctly, it affects the whole system. Either the thermostat needs to be replaced or there's a bigger issue with the equipment.
  • Dirty filter. A dirty filter restricts airflow, so the system works harder to keep you comfortable and wears out sooner.
  • Clogged or noisy drain. A clogged condensate drain can lead to water damage in your ceiling. We clear the drain and get the water flowing where it belongs.
  • Dirty or blocked coils. Dirt and debris on the coils cut efficiency, make the system run longer, and lead to higher bills and breakdowns.
  • Low refrigerant. If the system runs but doesn't cool the house well, it may be low on refrigerant, which leads to long run times and failed components.
  • Refrigerant leaks. A leak means the system has to work harder than it should to cool your home. Leaks need to be found and repaired, not just topped off.
  • Uneven cooling. Hot and cold spots and long run times can point to refrigerant, airflow, or duct problems.
  • Broken compressor. The compressor is the heart of the cooling system. If it stops, the cooling stops.
  • Overheating. If your AC keeps shutting itself off, the cause can be as simple as dirt or as serious as a failing part.
  • Damaged coils. Damaged coils can cause the system to overheat. They need to be repaired if possible, or replaced.
