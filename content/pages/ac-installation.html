AC Replacement & Installation

Sometimes repair isn't the answer. When your system needs to be replaced, including a replacement authorized by your home warranty, Kappa-Air installs it right.

Kappa-Air is a family-owned company that has provided quality air conditioning installation for more than 15 years. We install all types of systems, including split systems, package units, ductless systems, gas, electric, and heat pumps.

Repair or replace?

Deciding whether to replace an air conditioner can be difficult. We give you, and your warranty company if you have one, a detailed report on the system's current condition and likely future issues, so the decision is an informed one.

Installed correctly

A new air conditioner has to be installed correctly. Done poorly, it won't cool your home properly, can create safety hazards, and can damage the unit. We screen every Kappa-Air employee to make sure they're skilled, honest, and dependable, so your new system works the way it should.

Choosing the right size

Correct sizing is critical to how a new system performs and how efficiently it runs. Our process:

  • Load calculation. We look at insulation, windows, doors, walls, and more to size the system to your home.
  • Room-by-room walkthrough. We assess how each space is used so the system fits how you actually live.
  • Duct evaluation. We check the air distribution system and its layout, so we can address hot and cold spots.

When a replacement is covered by your home warranty, the equipment options are set by your warranty company and your plan. We'll walk you through what's been approved and answer your questions.

Our installation process

Once your cooling needs are determined and a properly sized unit is chosen, our technicians work quickly and carefully to get the new system running. Some installations require moving ductwork or wiring; we handle every part of the job for a smooth installation.
