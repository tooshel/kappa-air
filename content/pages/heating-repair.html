Signs your furnace needs repair

  • Unusual noises. Rattling, grinding, or buzzing from the furnace or vents.
  • Strange odors. Smells from your vents can indicate a problem with the furnace or ducts. Turn the system off, open windows, and call a professional if the smell continues.
  • Slow to heat. If you don't feel warmth soon after turning on the heat, a part such as the blower motor may be failing.
  • Dirty filters. A clogged filter makes your furnace run inefficiently and can cause it to overheat.
  • Thermostat trouble. A faulty thermostat can keep the furnace from running correctly and drive up your utility bills.
  • Frequent breakdowns. If you keep needing service calls, it's time for a thorough diagnosis.

If any of these sound familiar, we can help. We'll inspect your heating system, determine what repairs are needed, and make sure it's running safely and efficiently.
