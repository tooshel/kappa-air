Duct cleaning and inspection

Your system depends on clean, clear ductwork. Dust and debris make it run inefficiently, and blocked ducts keep air from circulating, which can lead to moisture buildup in your home. We inspect your ductwork and clear any obstructions.

10 common heating problems

  • Faulty thermostat. A thermostat that isn't working correctly affects the whole system, or points to a bigger issue with the equipment.
  • Dirty filter. Poor airflow can make a heating system overheat and shut down, wearing out major parts early.
  • Noise. Rattling, grinding, or buzzing from the unit means something isn't operating correctly.
  • Poor airflow. Dirt in the system, poor ductwork, closed registers, and dirty filters all reduce airflow and performance.
  • Slow to heat. If you don't feel warmth soon after turning on the heat, a heating component may be damaged or failing.
  • Short cycling. A system that turns on and off in short bursts needs attention right away.
  • Refrigerant leaks. In a heat pump, leaks make the system defrost too often and work harder than it should.
  • Dirt. Dirty coils, blower wheels, and flame sensors are among the most common causes of heating and cooling trouble.
  • Ventilation. Furnaces need proper venting to run safely. If something is blocking it, call us.
  • Odors. Strange smells from your vents can point to a duct problem. Turn the system off, open windows for fresh air, and call a professional if the smell continues.
