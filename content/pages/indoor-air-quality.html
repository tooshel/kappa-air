Indoor Air Quality

Cleaner air means a healthier, more comfortable home. Kappa-Air helps homes across the Valley breathe easier.

Poor indoor air quality can contribute to allergies, asthma, headaches, fatigue, coughing, sneezing, and other respiratory issues. If you've been noticing these symptoms at home, it may be time to look at the air you're breathing.

Indoor air quality services

We'll evaluate your home on site to find the type of air purification system that fits it best. Common systems we install include:

  • HEPA filtration
  • Carbon filters
  • Ionizers
  • UV germicidal lamps
  • Humidifiers and dehumidifiers
  • Moisture sensors

Our team has more than 15 years of experience installing and servicing all types of HVAC equipment. We also provide duct cleaning and dryer vent cleaning.

What causes poor indoor air quality?

Contaminants in the air can come from many sources: building materials, furniture, carpets, pets, cooking, mold, dust mites, pollen, chemicals, paints, and pesticides. If you suspect your air isn't what it should be, we can help you find the source and recommend solutions.

Ways to improve your indoor air

  • Keep your home clean. Regular cleaning cuts down on dust, dander, and allergens.
  • Ventilate. Fresh air helps keep indoor pollutants from building up.
  • Find the source of odors. Some come from volatile organic compounds (VOCs) in paint, carpeting, and cleaners.
  • Add filtration. An air purifier or upgraded filtration removes allergens and irritants, which especially helps with allergies and asthma.
  • Consider a whole-house system. Whole-house ventilation and filtration treat the air in every room, not just one.

Have a home warranty? Air quality upgrades usually aren't part of a warranty repair, but we're happy to talk about options for your home while we're there.
